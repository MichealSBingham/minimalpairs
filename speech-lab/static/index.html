Transfer

Switch to another pair with the same vowels. A successful practice attempt does not yet establish learning.

MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED

Can we trust the feedback?

These are small engineering experiments. The human word recordings have source labels, but have not received an independent phonetic listening review. Learner sentences have published expert ratings.

The phoneme model is a research component. We have not validated live microphone accuracy, regional accent scoring, or learning outcomes.

THE NEXT LAYER

Build a target accent. Keep your identity.

A useful accent coach should compare specific choices with an accent you select. It should explain those choices rather than claim where you are from.

Vowel profile

Repeated, labeled vowel samples and trajectories, normalized for the speaker. This lab is the first experiment.

Accent features

Rhoticity, vowel mergers, consonant patterns, rhythm and intonation. Each needs reviewed references and its own validation.

Performance

Practice an unfamiliar phrase, with the chart hidden. Check consistency and listener judgments over time.

Regional accent classification is not implemented. The existing data does not establish which accent your speech resembles. General American and a specific contemporary southern British target are candidate first packs, subject to demand and data checks.
