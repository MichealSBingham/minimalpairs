# MinimalPairs product contract

Owner clarification, 28 September 2026. Use this to review changes to the rescue build.

## Core listening task

- Default to audio-only ABX discrimination. A and B are references; the learner matches X to a reference category by listening. Word identification can be a separately labeled optional mode; it must not replace ABX.
- Hide word spellings, IPA, meanings and revealing accessible labels during unassisted trials. Text requires an explicit learner action. Record any text assistance for the whole session.
- Require successful A, B and X playback before answering. Preserve replay, keyboard and mobile access.
- The current cross-word variant uses a different word for X and preserves the relevant target position/category. This is a product design choice, not a claim that a cited ABX study tested precisely this variant.

## Voices and actual recordings

- Draw the correct A/B position independently of target category, speaker assignment and previous answers. Do not enforce answer quotas whose remaining answers are inferable.
- With at least two available voice IDs, use one ID for both A/B references and a different ID for X; choose independently of the answer. A one-voice device fallback is limited preview practice, not evidence of cross-speaker training.
- Voice IDs alone do not establish acoustic speaker identity. A release claiming varied speakers needs source-backed real speaker attribution or stable synthetic conditioning, plus listening checks. Per-word VoiceDesign prompts do not yet meet that requirement.
- Audit pronunciation, dialect, naturalness, token variation and recording-condition artifacts. Metadata, codec and checksum checks are engineering checks, not listening approval.
- Repeat practice can reuse cached tokens. Do not claim it prevents recording memorization or proves generalization. Evaluate transfer separately with genuinely held-out words and speakers, with no text assistance or earlier training exposure.
- Generated audio remains labeled generated. Human source recordings retain their individual provenance, licenses and review status. No voice cloning of a real person is authorized by this contract.

## Discovery and retention

- Protect existing search routes and prioritize demonstrated query demand. A landing page must offer useful information and a relevant functioning exercise.
- Let a learner experience complete guest practice before requiring an account or payment.
- Make completion feedback and the next suggested practice clear. Preserve history and offer account saving explicitly.
- Short sessions, review intervals, gamification, prices and upgrade timing are hypotheses until measured. Do not present a practice score as proficiency or a commercial forecast as a promise.
- Measure starts, successful first answers, completions, playback failures and completed returns with coherent, mature denominators. Optional analytics only represents consenting browsers. More pages, features or agents do not establish retention.

## Operating constraints

Keep incremental operating cost near zero. Preserve historical lifetime entitlements. No acquisition, support or transactional email is sent before the owner's final communication approval. Free service configuration, local work and research continue independently. Keep staged, deployed, source-checked, listening-reviewed and outcome-validated work distinct in reports.
