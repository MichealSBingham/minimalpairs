# Speaking research continuity

For pronunciation, vowel maps, accent assessment, or voice-coach work, read
`SPEECH-ROADMAP.md` and `speech-lab/STATUS.md` first. They record the agreed
sequence, current stage, evidence, budget, and next experiment. Update the
status after completed experiments; advance stages only on the stated evidence.

Keep experimental speech work isolated from the listening product until
integration is requested. Preserve historical results and record new experiments
in dated directories. Distinguish measurement error, pronunciation correctness,
and learning outcomes. Previously inspected corpus recordings are not an
independent holdout.
